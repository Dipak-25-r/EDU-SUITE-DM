10.2 Position Vector & Direction Cosines

Representation in 3D Space $(x, y, z)$

  • Position Vector ($\vec{OP}$): For point $P(x, y, z)$ relative to origin $O(0, 0, 0)$:
    $\vec{OP} = x\hat{i} + y\hat{j} + z\hat{k}$  |  $|\vec{OP}| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$
  • Direction Angles & Cosines: Angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ with axes $X, Y, Z$.
    $l = \cos\alpha = \frac{x}{r}$
    $m = \cos\beta = \frac{y}{r}$
    $n = \cos\gamma = \frac{z}{r}$
  • Fundamental Identity: $l^2 + m^2 + n^2 = 1$

🏷️ 10.3 Classification & Types of Vectors

0️⃣ Null Vector (શૂન્ય સદિશ)

Initial & terminal points coincide ($\vec{0}$). Magnitude is $0$, direction is arbitrary.

🎯 Unit Vector (એકમ સદિશ)

Magnitude is exactly $1$ unit. Normalized form: $\hat{a} = \frac{\vec{a}}{|\vec{a}|}$.

📍 Co-initial Vectors (સમઉદ્ભવ)

Two or more vectors sharing the exact same starting/initial point.

📏 Collinear Vectors (સમરેખ)

Vectors parallel to the same straight line, regardless of magnitudes or direction senses.

⚖️ Equal Vectors (સમાન સદિશો)

Vectors having both identical magnitude AND direction ($\vec{a} = \vec{b}$).

🔄 Negative Vector (ઋણ સદિશ)

Vector with equal magnitude but strictly opposite direction ($\vec{BA} = -\vec{AB}$).

🔺 10.4 Geometric Laws of Vector Addition

Triangle Law of Addition (ત્રિકોણનો નિયમ)

If two vectors are represented by two sides of a triangle taken in order, their sum is represented by the third side taken in reverse order.

$\vec{AB} + \vec{BC} = \vec{AC} \implies \vec{AB} + \vec{BC} + \vec{CA} = \vec{0}$

Parallelogram Law (સમાંતરબાજુ ચતુષ્કોણ)

If two co-initial vectors represent adjacent sides of a parallelogram, their resultant is the diagonal passing through their common initial point.

$\vec{OA} + \vec{OB} = \vec{OC} \quad (\text{since } \vec{AC} = \vec{OB})$

✖️ Dot Product ($\cdot$) vs Cross Product ($\times$)

Feature 🔵 Scalar / Dot Product ($\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$) 🔴 Vector / Cross Product ($\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$)
Result Type Scalar Real Number Vector (has magnitude & direction)
Definition Formula $|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$ $|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta \cdot \hat{n}$
Commutativity $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} = \vec{b}\cdot\vec{a}$ (Yes) $\vec{a}\times\vec{b} = -(\vec{b}\times\vec{a})$ (Anti)
Zero Result Condition $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} = 0 \iff \vec{a} \perp \vec{b}$ (Perpendicular) $\vec{a}\times\vec{b} = \vec{0} \iff \vec{a} \parallel \vec{b}$ (Parallel)
Unit Vectors ($\hat{i},\hat{j},\hat{k}$) $\hat{i}\cdot\hat{i} = \hat{j}\cdot\hat{j} = \hat{k}\cdot\hat{k} = 1$
$\hat{i}\cdot\hat{j} = \hat{j}\cdot\hat{k} = \hat{k}\cdot\hat{i} = 0$ 		$\hat{i}\times\hat{i} = \hat{j}\times\hat{j} = \hat{k}\times\hat{k} = \vec{0}$
$\hat{i}\times\hat{j} = \hat{k}, \quad \hat{j}\times\hat{k} = \hat{i}, \quad \hat{k}\times\hat{i} = \hat{j}$
Component Matrix Calculation $a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3$ $\begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \end{vmatrix}$
Geometric Application Angle between vectors & projection Triangle area ($\frac{1}{2}|\vec{a}\times\vec{b}|$) & Parallelogram area
